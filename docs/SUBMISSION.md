# Codegeist 2026 submission checklist

Official pages: https://codegeist2026.devpost.com/ and https://codegeist2026.devpost.com/rules . Registration/submission closes 22 December 2026 at 12:00 Pacific Time (22:00 South Africa time). Verify timezone on Devpost near submission. Category: Apps for Business Teams. First prize is per category. Forge app must use at least one Atlassian product/capability; this project targets Jira.

## Required evidence
- [ ] Register/join on Devpost and obtain a free Atlassian Cloud developer site.
- [ ] Build an original Forge app with manifest and module; connect Jira and show real functionality.
- [ ] Install in clean judge test environment from sharing/installation link; document all setup and sample data steps in docs/INSTALL.md.
- [ ] Record a public YouTube demo under five minutes showing actual functioning app and end-to-end scenario.
- [ ] Provide project description, Forge/Jira technology explanation, category and selected bonus details in English.
- [ ] Complete all Devpost form fields before deadline; check app and video remain accessible during judging.
- [ ] Confirm ownership and permissions for third-party SDKs/assets; no unlicensed music or trademarks in video.

Bonus entries require their actual integration: Rovo agent module or reusable skills/actions for Best Agents; rovo:mcp module exposing tools for Atlassian MCP; Teamwork Graph connection/API use; Marketplace listing or review ticket. These are optional and must not be claimed on a design alone. Forge LLMs are optional and have no event credits.

Judging: stage-one viability/theme/API gate, then implementation, design, impact, innovation and presentation each 20%. Build a coherent working journey, including failed second leave request and recovered coverage. Rules take precedence over this checklist.
